Rare Flowers & The Frequency

There's a version of every song that starts before it's a song.

Before the studio, before the mix, before anybody else hears it — it's just a voice and a guitar and whatever you were going through when it came out of you.

That's where Rare Flowers starts.

This project holds the songs that came from the ground up — real sessions, real moments, no filters. Some of these tracks were written during a stretch where I was losing things I thought I couldn't survive losing: a place to stay, a relationship, a vehicle, people I called friends. What I didn't lose was the belief I had in myself — and if anything, that period is what locked it in.

Rare Flowers is built on a simple idea: the most powerful things often come from the harshest environments. Like a flower breaking through concrete, finding light where nothing was supposed to grow. Each song is a different flower in that garden — a memory, a scar, a victory, a relationship, a moment that shaped who I am. It's Soul Rock at its core: raw acoustic expression fused with real storytelling.

This one isn't about where I started. It's about what you become when you refuse to stop growing. 7-8 tracks, all rooted in that truth.

Right alongside it, there's The Frequency

The other side of what I build. This is the AI-driven universe where Zeal Von Rebel and DJ Zuri Wave live, part of a bigger collective and a bigger story that's been unfolding behind the scenes.

Zeal's debut single, "Iron In My Chest," drops July 23rd — the first real look into that world. From there, The Frequency expands into a full project of its own, 10+ tracks deep, blending music, visuals, and story in a way that hasn't really been done from where I sit.

The Convergence

Here's the part that ties it all together: one song is going to exist in both worlds. The same record, recorded in my own voice for Rare Flowers, and reimagined through Zeal Von Rebel for The Frequency.
